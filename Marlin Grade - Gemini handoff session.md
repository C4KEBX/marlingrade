\f0\fs24 \cf0 # Marlin Grade - Brand Strategy & Product Blueprint\
\
## 1. Core Identity & Concept\
*   **Registered Legal Name:** Marlin Grade\
*   **Marketing Brand Name:** Marlin\
*   **Core Value Proposition:** A B2B real estate predictive intelligence engine that aggregates scattered, unorganized public record data to score the propensity of individual residential addresses to go on the market.\
*   **Target Audience:** Solo real estate agents and small boutique teams looking to optimize their geographic farming budgets.\
*   **Algorithm Strategy:** Grades properties from A to F based on localized public data points (e.g., historical tenure, owner occupancy vs. institutional/rental status, tax assessments).\
\
---\
\
## 2. B2B Brand Persona\
*   **The Vibe:** The "Apex Predator" of local real estate tech\'97fast, precise, sleek, and highly efficient.\
*   **Visual Palette:** Premium enterprise SaaS aesthetics. Matte blacks, deep ocean blues, and a sharp, neon "Data Green" or "Signal Cyan" for the predictive grades.\
*   **Brand Voice:** Performance-driven, sharp, and ROI-focused. Speaks like a business builder rather than a corporate spreadsheet.\
\
---\
\
## 3. Core Product Features (MVP)\
*   **The Marlin Grade:** An address scoring system (A\'96F) evaluating listing likelihood.\
*   **Database Search:** Instant lookups for individual addresses.\
*   **Zip Code / Farm Area Monitoring:** Automated tracking with scheduled reporting and instant alert triggers when a property score spikes.\
*   **Data Quality Filter:** Automatic downgrading of corporate rental properties to protect agents from wasting marketing spend on institutional landlords.\
*   *Note: Custom polygon map drawing is reserved for post-MVP phase two development.*\
\
---\
\

## 5. Regional Expansion Strategy (Texas-First Roadmap)\
*   **Phase 1:** Dominate Austin, TX (Hyper-local validation and refining scraping speed).\
*   **Phase 2:** Expand to contiguous major Texas metros: Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Houston, and San Antonio.\
*   **Growth Mechanic:** Deploy regional landing pages (e.g., `/dfw`, `/houston`) to collect waitlist sign-ups. Gamify the launch by allocating data scraping priorities to the zip codes with the highest concentration of waiting agents.\
